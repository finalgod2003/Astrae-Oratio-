Recruitment for the Astrae Oratio closed beta ended on **Monday, September 21, 2026 at 12:00 (UTC+9)**. Two days later, on **September 23**, NC began sending selection emails to chosen testers in batches and confirmed the test dates:

- **CBT period:** October 2, 2026 (Fri) 12:00 – October 6, 2026 (Tue) 12:00, UTC+9.
- **Notification:** only selected applicants received an email; NC advised checking spam folders.
- **Pre-download:** testers later received a pre-download email on October 2 at 10:00 (UTC+9).

Applicants who were not selected did not get a separate notice. A small number of guaranteed slots also went to winners of the official **Sign-Up Verification Event** on X, announced on September 21.

The test has since finished — read our [CBT wrap-up](/news/global-cbt-wrap-up-october-2026/) for what was in it.
