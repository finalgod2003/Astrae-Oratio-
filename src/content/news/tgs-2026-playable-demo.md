Astrae Oratio was **playable for the first time** at **Tokyo Game Show 2026**, held September 17–21 at Makuhari Messe in Chiba, Japan. NC ran a dedicated booth for the game.

## What was at the booth

- A **playable demo** with a choice of a **scenario build** or a **combat build**. Media reports put the demo's story section at the prologue and the Minato Arc, with **12 playable magicians**.
- A **Prologue Scenario Screening** on the booth stage.
- Giveaways for visitors who tried the demo or followed the official accounts, including lenticular cards, character keychains, Special District Office business-card cases, gym sacks and hand fans.

## Prologue Digest PV

NC also released the **Prologue Digest PV** on **September 17**, a video summary of the opening story. It's embedded above.

## Interviews

At the show, the developers explained their combat philosophy — prediction and planning over reflexes, with **Dominion Skills** and **Artifact Liberation** as the dramatic turning points — and said they are aiming for a **simultaneous global launch**. We've folded those details into the [combat guide](/guides/combat/).
