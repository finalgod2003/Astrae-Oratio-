**Astrae Oratio** — previously codenamed *Project AT* — stepped out of the shadows in spring 2026. Its teaser website went up at the end of April, counting down to the reveal, and on **May 7, 2026** NC posted its official welcome notice and released the **Super Teaser PV**.

## What was revealed

- **Developer:** Dynamis One, a studio NC invested in in January 2026 and the team behind the cancelled *Project KV*.
- **Publisher:** NC, globally.
- **Genre:** a "Shindenki" magic RPG built around **magic and public administration**.
- **Premise:** you play the [Shunin](/characters/shunin/), an ordinary civil servant transferred to the [Special District Office](/factions/#special-district-office), the agency that quietly manages Tokyo's magicians.

On **May 12**, the official site added its first character profiles and world-setting pages, starting with the Special District Office cast: Commissioner [Hiwagishiakari Ai](/characters/hiwagishiakari-ai/), the wooden bodyguard [Doll](/characters/doll/) and the mascot Hoshi-chan. New characters were then added over the following months.

For the setting itself — Tokyo in 1889, about a century ahead of real history — see our [world guide](/world/).
