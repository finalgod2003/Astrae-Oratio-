NC opened applications for the **Astrae Oratio closed beta test** on **August 19, 2026 at 03:00 UTC**, with sign-ups running until **September 21, 2026 at 03:00 UTC**. Applying meant filling in a survey on the official website; testers were then picked by lottery.

## CBT at a glance

- **Platforms:** Android and iOS (no emulators).
- **Region:** global, with possible restrictions in some regions for network or stability reasons.
- **Text languages:** English, Japanese, Korean, Traditional Chinese. **Voice:** Japanese.
- **Age:** 16 or older (guardian consent required where local law treats you as a minor).
- **Purchases:** disabled. **Data:** wiped after the test.
- **Device lock:** access was tied to the first device you logged in with; switching devices got the account suspended for the rest of the test.
- **Storage:** at least 10 GB free recommended.

## Device requirements

| | Android (min.) | Android (rec.) | iOS (min.) | iOS (rec.) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chip | Snapdragon 7 Gen 1 | Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 | A14 | A16 |
| RAM | 6 GB | 8 GB | 4 GB | 6 GB |
| OS | Android 12 | Android 14 | iOS 16.0 | iOS 18.0 |

These specs were for the in-development CBT build and may change for launch. We keep the latest version on the [release date & download page](/release-date-download/#requirements).

## Sign-Up Verification Event

From September 1, NC also ran an X campaign: post a screenshot of your completed application with **#AsoraCBT** for a chance at one of **20 guaranteed tester slots** across all official channels.
