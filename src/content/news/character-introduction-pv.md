NC released the **Character Introduction PV** — titled *City, Stars, and Magic* — on **June 23, 2026**. It was the most complete look at the game up to that point, mixing character introductions with gameplay: battle scenes, the cast relaxing in school swimsuits, and samples of the story presentation.

## Who appears

The PV introduced the clans revealed so far, including the [Magical Activities of the Minato Girls](/factions/#minato-girls), the [Seiran Fellowship](/factions/#seiran-fellowship) and the [Akina Urban Legend Research Institute](/factions/#akina-institute). It also teased a then-unnamed magician from **Kyoto** in traditional dress — later revealed as [Kujo Yuria](/characters/kujo-yuria/).

## Context

By the time of the PV, the official teaser site had already published **12 character profiles**, **13 comics** and **four sets of SD stamps**. Battle footage had first been shown on June 2. Japanese coverage also highlighted the team behind the game: Dynamis One is led by Park Byung-rim, formerly a producer on *Blue Archive*, and scenario writer isakusan also previously worked on that game.
