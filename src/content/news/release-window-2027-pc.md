Ahead of Tokyo Game Show 2026, NC and developer Dynamis One gave Astrae Oratio its first release window on **September 16, 2026**:

- **iOS and Android:** 2027.
- **PC:** confirmed for **after** the mobile launch; no date yet.
- **Voice acting:** the main story is fully voiced in **Japanese**.

No exact launch date, price model or pre-registration campaign was announced. The developers have said they are aiming for a **simultaneous global release** across Asia, North America and Europe, rather than a staggered regional rollout, and that they see the game as an ongoing "serialized" story.
