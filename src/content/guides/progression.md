This page maps out what you actually do in Astrae Oratio day to day — story, battles, social time with the cast — and how characters get stronger. It is based on the **October 2026 CBT**, so names and numbers may still change.

## Story and Scenario Packs {#story}

The story is told **visual-novel style**, with dialogue choices, character art, cut-ins and **full Japanese voice acting** (Tokyo Game Show coverage says the voice work extends to monologues, not just dialogue). Story chapters are grouped into **Scenario Packs**.

The CBT contained three main scenarios and two shorter mini stories:

| Scenario | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Prologue — *Tokyo, Civil Servants, and Magicians* (title as translated in press coverage) | The [Shunin](/characters/shunin/)'s transfer to the Special District Office |
| Minato Arc Chapter 1 — *The Sparkling Days of the Minato Magicians* | [Magical Activities of the Minato Girls](/factions/#minato-girls) |
| Shinjuku Arc Chapter 1 — *The Night of Urban Legend Hunters and the Vanished Magician* | [Akina Urban Legend Research Institute](/factions/#akina-institute) |

Clearing each arc chapter in the CBT rewarded **1,000 Meteorites and 60 Starlight Films**. One player write-up estimated the prologue alone at about **an hour of reading** across eight episodes, so the story is substantial.

> Voice acting for Episodes 5–7 of Minato Arc Chapter 1 was still in production during the CBT. NC says the full main story will be voiced at launch.

## Battle quests and difficulty {#quests}

Battle quests in the CBT took place in **Minato** and **Shinjuku**, each with four difficulty levels: **Normal, Hard, Very Hard and Challenge**. See the [combat guide](/guides/combat/) for how battles work.

## Score Trial {#score-trial}

**Score Trial** pits you against bosses under **varying conditions**, scoring your performance. Seasonal Score Trials are the main source of **Starlight Films**, the currency for the [Vignette Gacha](/guides/gacha/#vignette-gacha) — so expect this to be the mode competitive players care about most.

## Simulation Room {#simulation-room}

The **Simulation Room** is a dungeon for farming **growth materials**. Entry uses **Simulation Room Tickets**; the CBT login rewards gave eight per day.

## Raid {#raid}

Press coverage of the CBT announcement mentioned **newly revealed raid content**. No official details about its rules or rewards have been published yet.

## Free Time {#free-time}

**Free Time** is the social side of the game. On a map of Tokyo you can visit the **23 special wards and the surrounding suburbs** to meet magicians and see slices of their daily life, raising your **bond level** with them.

- Each visit spends **Free Time Points**.
- Each visit also **advances the clock** — from day to night or from night to day — so who you can meet depends on timing.

The CBT also included **costumes** that change a character's appearance, plus character voice lines to unlock.

## Training {#training}

**Training** is the game's built-in tutorial. **Basic Training** covers the fundamentals of combat; **Advanced Training** explains how to use specific characters in more detail. In the CBT the mode was presented as Riria and Anna's after-school magic training. If you're learning a new character, start here.

## Great Shrine and Doll {#great-shrine}

[Doll](/characters/doll/) is the one character you're guaranteed to get without the gacha. You restore Doll by **leveling up the Great Shrine** (also called the Grand Temple); repairs use **Oracle Device Daisy Wheels**. Once restoration finishes, Doll joins your roster.

## How characters get stronger {#growth}

From the CBT and player reports, character growth has at least three layers:

1. **Levels** — raised with Magician EXP.
2. **Skills** — players reported incremental skill upgrades.
3. **Vignettes** — party-wide equipment cards from the Vignette Gacha.

Bond levels from Free Time are a fourth track, though whether they give combat bonuses hasn't been confirmed. We'll publish full upgrade-cost tables once the launch build is out.

## Does CBT progress carry over? {#carry-over}

No. The CBT wiped all data — characters, currency and story progress — when it ended on October 6, 2026, and nothing transfers to launch.
