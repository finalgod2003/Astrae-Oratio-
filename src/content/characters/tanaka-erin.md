**Tanaka Erin** (田中えりん) is a member of the [Magical Activities of the Minato Girls](/factions/#minato-girls) and one of the faces of Astrae Oratio's marketing. She is voiced by **Nanami Hinami** (七海日南).

## Profile

Erin is an ordinary Japanese middle schooler, born and raised in Japan. Her mother is of Irish descent, so Erin looks unmistakably Western — but in every other way she is a regular Japanese girl, and she cannot actually speak English.

Her life stayed completely normal until middle school, when she happened to meet [Fujiwara Riria](/characters/fujiwara-riria/) and [Anna M. Battenberg](/characters/anna-m-battenberg/). Through them she learned that she can use an **ancient form of mythic magic** that everyone believed was lost. Since then the three have lived a secret life as magicians, getting tangled up in magician incidents all over Tokyo.

## Story role

Erin is at the center of the **Minato Arc**. Coverage of early gameplay footage describes her as a girl who has only recently become a magician and is still missing a lot of basic knowledge about that world — which makes her a natural guide for players who are new to it too. In the October 2026 CBT, the arc's first chapter was called *The Sparkling Days of the Minato Magicians*.

## In combat

Erin carried the **Breaker** role label in the CBT build — a role that, by its name, focuses on wearing down enemy [Break gauges](/guides/combat/#break). Official role definitions have not been published yet.

## Relationships

- [Fujiwara Riria](/characters/fujiwara-riria/) and [Anna M. Battenberg](/characters/anna-m-battenberg/) — her clanmates and the friends who introduced her to magic.
- The [Shunin](/characters/shunin/) — the public official in charge of her ward's magicians.
