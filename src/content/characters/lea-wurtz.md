**Léa Wurtz** (レア・ヴュルツ) is the proprietress of [Cafe Ichigo](/factions/#cafe-ichigo). No voice actor has been listed for her yet.

## Profile

Léa looks genuinely intimidating — enough that she has apparently assumed the [Shunin](/characters/shunin/) is an expert in running shady, not-quite-legal businesses. She is a **retired magician**.

Her management style is famously hands-off. She leaves almost everything to [Natsume Souka](/characters/natsume-souka/) and spends her days reading the newspaper. She is also the person solely responsible for turning Cafe Ichigo into an **animal-themed maid café**.

That relaxed approach has had an interesting side effect: Ichigo has become a gathering place for **ordinary people and magicians alike**. Whether she planned it that way is anyone's guess.

## In the game

Léa was not among the 18 characters playable in the October 2026 CBT footage.
