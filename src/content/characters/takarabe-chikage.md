**Takarabe Chikage** (財部千景) is the leader of the [Rose of Bunkyo](/factions/#rose-of-bunkyo), a vigilante group that keeps the peace in Bunkyo ward. She is voiced by **Kawaguchi Sakura** (川口桜).

## Profile

Chikage got into the **University of Tokyo** through years of relentless study, and she is very proud of it — she will happily remind you that a Todai student can help with anything.

As a magician she uses **topological magic** built on her scientific knowledge, and she puts her considerable ability to work keeping order on Bunkyo's hidden side.

## In combat

Chikage was the **only Defender** among the 18 characters playable in the October 2026 CBT, which makes her a character to watch once team-building guides become possible. See [combat roles](/guides/combat/#roles).
