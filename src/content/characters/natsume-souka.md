**Natsume Souka** (夏目漱花) works at [Cafe Ichigo](/factions/#cafe-ichigo). She is voiced by **Inagaki Konomi** (稲垣好).

## Profile

Souka is Cafe Ichigo's **barista, server and cook** all at once — in practice, the person who keeps the place running while the owner reads the paper. She looks young, but she is actually a **university student close to graduation**.

She started out as a regular customer and somehow ended up on staff. Since Ichigo is an animal-themed maid café, she wears ears and a tail — accessories, she is quick to point out, that will not move no matter how long you stare.

Underneath the breezy service voice she is sensitive and thoughtful, and she seems to be quietly worrying about what comes after graduation.

Souka carried the **Breaker** role label in the October 2026 CBT. See [combat roles](/guides/combat/#roles).
