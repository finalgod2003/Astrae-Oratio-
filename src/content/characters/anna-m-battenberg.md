**Anna M. Battenberg** (アンナ・M・バッテンベルク) is a member of the [Magical Activities of the Minato Girls](/factions/#minato-girls). Her full name is **Anna Marie von Battenberg**. She is voiced by **Takahashi Minami** (髙橋ミナミ).

## Profile

Anna is a young noblewoman from **Vienna**. Her family has a peculiar obsession: ever since her grandfather's generation grew tired of the family's old-fashioned traditions, "finding a suitable match" has become the household's top priority. The result is a family that watches Anna's love life far too closely, to her obvious irritation.

In Tokyo she lives in a building with a **flower shop**, run by her family's retired butler. For reasons of his own, the butler has decided the [Shunin](/characters/shunin/) might make a fine future son-in-law.

Anna arrived expecting a quiet school life. Meeting [Tanaka Erin](/characters/tanaka-erin/) and [Fujiwara Riria](/characters/fujiwara-riria/) put an end to that — though, by her own account, she doesn't mind as much as she expected.

## In combat

Anna carried the **Attacker** role label in the October 2026 CBT, making her one of the Minato trio's damage dealers alongside Erin's Breaker kit and Riria's support. See [combat roles](/guides/combat/#roles).

## Relationships

- [Tanaka Erin](/characters/tanaka-erin/) and [Fujiwara Riria](/characters/fujiwara-riria/) — clanmates and classmates.
- The [Shunin](/characters/shunin/) — her butler's preferred candidate for her hand, whether she likes it or not.
