**Hanamori Moa** (花盛百杏) is a member of the [Rose of Bunkyo](/factions/#rose-of-bunkyo). She is voiced by **Amami Yurina** (天海由梨奈).

## Profile

By night, Moa patrols Bunkyo with the Rose of Bunkyo — and for reasons nobody quite explains, she insists on calling herself a **phantom thief** and is determined to live out the full romance of the role, down to promising to steal the [Shunin](/characters/shunin/)'s heart.

By day she is a diligent student at a **prestigious women's university for education**.
