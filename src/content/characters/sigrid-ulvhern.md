**Sigrid Ulvhern** is one of the 18 characters that were playable in the Astrae Oratio global CBT (October 2–6, 2026). There is **no official profile yet**: as of this update the official character page does not list Sigrid, and the clan, Japanese name and voice actor are all unannounced.

## What we know

- **Role label in the CBT:** Sweeper. See [combat roles](/guides/combat/#roles).
- **Name spelling:** taken from the CBT's English build as shown in community gameplay footage. The official romanization may differ.
- **Clan:** not revealed. The name stands out in a mostly Japanese cast, but the game already features magicians from abroad (Vienna-born Anna, Florence-born Vita Neri), so that alone says little about Sigrid's affiliation.

## Why Sigrid isn't on the official site yet

The CBT featured 18 playable characters, while the official character page profiles a different, only partly overlapping set. A few magicians were effectively revealed by the test itself. Official profiles have so far arrived in batches, so an introduction before launch is likely.

We will update this page with clan, voice actor and kit details as soon as official information is published.
