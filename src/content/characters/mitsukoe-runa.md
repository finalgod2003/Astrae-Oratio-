**Mitsukoe Runa** (三声瑠奈) is a member of the [Seiran Fellowship](/factions/#seiran-fellowship). She is voiced by **Yamada Misuzu** (山田美鈴).

> **Name note:** the official site's internal ID for her is "luna", but her official English name is spelled **Runa**.

## Profile

Runa is the only daughter of the **Mitsukoe family**, a real-estate dynasty that joined the ranks of the super-rich almost overnight thanks to an unprecedented property boom — a nod to the bubble-era feel of [Tokyo '89](/world/#tokyo-89).

Money did not change her grandfather: he insisted on staying true to his humble roots, which put him at odds with Runa's father, who wanted the luxury lifestyle. Runa was raised by her grandfather until he died when she was in the third grade; only then did she move in with her father and start living as an heiress.

She treasures her memories of her grandfather, and it shows: Runa has a soft spot for mature, older men. She has privately concluded that the [Shunin](/characters/shunin/) will be exactly her type — give or take ten years.
