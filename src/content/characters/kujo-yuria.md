**Kujo Yuria** (九条由理亜) is a magician from **Kyoto** affiliated with the [Mimawarigumi](/factions/#mimawarigumi). She is voiced by **Koiwai Kotori** (小岩井ことり).

## Profile

Yuria's official profile is short and pointed: she has come to Tokyo **to kill the [Shunin](/characters/shunin/)**, and little else about her is known. There is also a rumor that the Shunin stole her first kiss — which might explain a thing or two.

Her introduction line, delivered in Kyoto dialect, is an appraisal of how dazzling Tokyo's magic is. Given the backstory, there is probably an edge to it: in the game's world, Tokyo beat its rival Kyoto to host the World Expo after Paris (see [Tokyo '89](/world/#tokyo-89)).

When the Character PV was covered in June 2026, she had not yet been named and was described simply as a member of a mysterious Kyoto faction dressed in traditional Japanese clothing.

## About the Mimawarigumi

The name comes from the real **Mimawarigumi**, a shogunate police unit that patrolled Kyoto in the 1860s, alongside the better-known Shinsengumi. How closely the game's version follows that history has not been revealed.

## In the game

Yuria was **not** among the 18 characters playable in the October 2026 CBT footage.
