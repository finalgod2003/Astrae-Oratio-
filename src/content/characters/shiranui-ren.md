**Shiranui Ren** (不知火恋) is a member of the [Seiran Fellowship](/factions/#seiran-fellowship). She is voiced by **Kurosaki Shiori** (黒崎しおり).

## Profile

On the surface, Ren is the friend who follows [Mitsukoe Runa](/characters/mitsukoe-runa/) and [Keikou Mayoi](/characters/keikou-mayoi/) around — part supporter, part errand-runner, part friend. Their relationship is complicated, because the truth is that Ren is a **bodyguard and hitman** sent jointly by the Mitsukoe and Keikou families.

She is hard to read. Sometimes she seems to struggle with talking to people, sometimes she doesn't, and her official introduction line consists of a single ellipsis. Every so often she gives the [Shunin](/characters/shunin/) a quiet, predatory look.

## In combat

Ren carried the **Attacker** role label in the October 2026 CBT. See [combat roles](/guides/combat/#roles).
