**Asakura Mai** (朝倉真衣) is a member of the [Akina Urban Legend Research Institute](/factions/#akina-institute). She is voiced by **Yuikawa Asaki** (結川あさき).

## Profile

Mai is the **ace of her school's track-and-field club**, with her sights set on the Inter-High, Japan's national high school championships. She is a magician, but she has chosen to live as an ordinary girl and pour herself into athletics instead — she simply considers herself better at sports than at magic.

Quiet and stoic, she can come across as unapproachable. In reality she is a normal girl who loves **cute dolls**. She also has odd blind spots in common sense, such as going to class or wandering busy shopping districts in her workout clothes.

## In combat

Despite downplaying her magic, Mai carried the **Attacker** role label in the October 2026 CBT. See [combat roles](/guides/combat/#roles).
