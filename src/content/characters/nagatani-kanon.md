**Nagatani Kanon** is one of the 18 characters that were playable in the Astrae Oratio global CBT (October 2–6, 2026). There is **no official profile yet** — Kanon's clan, Japanese name (kanji) and voice actor have not been announced on the official site.

## What we know

- **Role label in the CBT:** Sweeper. See [combat roles](/guides/combat/#roles).
- **Name spelling:** taken from the CBT's English build as shown in community gameplay footage, in the same family-name-first order the official site uses. The official romanization may differ.
- **Clan:** not revealed.

## Why Kanon isn't on the official site yet

Several CBT characters were revealed by the test itself rather than through the official character page. An official profile before launch is likely; this page will be updated with clan, voice actor and kit details once it appears.
