**Enryu Koyomi** (淹留煌夜美) is a member of the [YuLuKo Trio](/factions/#yuluko-trio). She is voiced by **Hitomi Saya** (仁見紗綾).

## Profile

Koyomi is a fiery girl from **Aomori Prefecture**. Since childhood she has been drilled in witchcraft by her grandmother, **a great witch from Romania**.

She is fiercely proud of Aomori — and, at the same time, nursing an inferiority complex toward witches who grew up in the city. City life in Tokyo is not exactly easy on her. The moment a polished city witch stumbles at something Koyomi finds easy, though, she flashes a smug little grin and lays the condescension on thick. The official profile does not sugarcoat it: her personality can be exhausting.

## In combat

Koyomi was the only YuLuKo Trio member playable in the October 2026 CBT, where she carried the **Sweeper** role label. See [combat roles](/guides/combat/#roles).

## Relationships

- [Yanagihara Luise](/characters/yanagihara-luise/) — YuLuKo Trio member and Risara Academy troublemaker.
- [Aizumi Yuzuki](/characters/aizumi-yuzuki/) — YuLuKo Trio member from an ordinary Tokyo family, exactly the kind of "city witch" Koyomi feels complicated about.
