The **Shunin** (主任, roughly "chief" or "senior clerk") is the protagonist of Astrae Oratio and the role the player takes on. "Shunin" is a job title rather than a name, and the whole cast addresses the player that way.

## Who is the Shunin?

The Shunin starts the story as a low-ranking local public official in Tokyo — and, crucially, as an ordinary person. The official profile is explicit that he is **not a magician** and has no special abilities. Despite that, he is abruptly transferred to the [Special District Office](/factions/#special-district-office), the agency that administers every magic-related affair in Tokyo, and ends up as its only official employee.

From then on, every problem involving Tokyo's hidden magicians lands on his desk: territorial disputes between clans, incidents in the wards, and the odd attempt on his life.

## Role in the story

- **Boss:** [Hiwagishiakari Ai](/characters/hiwagishiakari-ai/), the Commissioner who arranged his transfer without asking.
- **Bodyguard:** [Doll](/characters/doll/), a wooden guardian the Commissioner built to keep him alive.
- **Support staff:** the chaotic [Dominion Management Council branch](/factions/#dominion-management-council) crew.
- **Mediator:** as a public official and dominion administrator, he is expected to settle disputes between magician clans, which the game frames as administrative work. See [Dominion](/world/#dominion).

## How other characters see him

The official profiles suggest the Shunin attracts a lot of attention for an "ordinary" clerk:

- [Kujo Yuria](/characters/kujo-yuria/) came from Kyoto specifically to kill him.
- [Vita Neri](/characters/vita-neri/) of the Ashen Dawn calls him the most dangerous man in Tokyo.
- [Mitsukoe Runa](/characters/mitsukoe-runa/) has decided he will be exactly her type — in about ten years.
- The retired butler looking after [Anna M. Battenberg](/characters/anna-m-battenberg/) treats him as a future son-in-law candidate.
- [Shiranui Ren](/characters/shiranui-ren/) occasionally watches him with a predatory look.

## Trivia

- The official site also lists a **"Shunin (Cursed)"** variant: one morning he wakes up from a nightmare and finds he has turned into a cat.
- Official English text refers to the Shunin as "he".
