**Vita Neri** (ビタ・ネリ) leads the Tokyo branch of the [Ashen Dawn](/factions/#ashen-dawn). The character is voiced by **Kazama Mayuko** (風間万裕子).

## Profile

The Ashen Dawn is a **secret society that lost a war of magic against Rome**. Defeated, its members scattered across the world, and they have been working to restore the society's former glory ever since.

Vita heads the group's **Tokyo branch**. The official profile reveals nothing beyond an origin in **Florence** — it doesn't even commit to pronouns. What it does make clear is Vita's view of the protagonist: the [Shunin](/characters/shunin/) is, in Vita's words, the **most dangerous man in the city**.

## In the game

Vita was **not** among the 18 characters playable in the October 2026 CBT footage. Everything so far points to an antagonist, although the official site has not labeled Vita as an enemy.
