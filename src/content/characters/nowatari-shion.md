**Nowatari Shion** is one of the 18 characters that were playable in the Astrae Oratio global CBT (October 2–6, 2026). Like [Sigrid Ulvhern](/characters/sigrid-ulvhern/) and [Nagatani Kanon](/characters/nagatani-kanon/), Shion has **no official profile yet**.

## What we know

- **Role label in the CBT:** Breaker. See [combat roles](/guides/combat/#roles) and the [Break mechanic](/guides/combat/#break).
- **Name spelling:** taken from the CBT's English build as shown in community gameplay footage. The official romanization may differ.
- **Clan, Japanese name and voice actor:** not announced.

## Why Shion isn't on the official site yet

The CBT roster and the official character page overlap only partly — the test revealed a few magicians before their official profiles were published. This page will be updated as soon as official information about Shion appears.
