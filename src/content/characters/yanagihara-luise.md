**Yanagihara Luise** (柳原ルイーゼ) is a member of the [YuLuKo Trio](/factions/#yuluko-trio), one of the most recently revealed groups. She is voiced by **Maruoka Wakana** (丸岡和佳奈).

## Profile

Luise attends **Risara Private Girls' Academy**, where she is known as a problem child. From the outside she looks like the picture of a refined young lady, but because she lacks the talent and skill her family expects, the **Yanagihara family considers her a disgrace**. Put simply: she is a troublemaker, and she is bored by anything that isn't either useful or fun — ideally something that would make her school or her family squirm.

Her saving grace is a strong sense of justice. She cannot walk past something she thinks is unfair, and when she sees someone looking down on others, she charges straight in and starts a fight.

## In the game

Luise was **not** among the 18 characters playable in the October 2026 CBT footage. Her playable status has not been announced.
