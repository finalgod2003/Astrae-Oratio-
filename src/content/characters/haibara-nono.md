**Haibara Nono** (灰原望乃) is a member of the [Rose of Bunkyo](/factions/#rose-of-bunkyo). She is voiced by **Miyazaki Hiyori** (宮崎ヒヨリ).

## Profile

Nono works as an **assistant nurse at a long-term care hospital**. The workload is brutal, and she lives in a near-permanent state of fatigue and stress — which may be why an unsettling smile sometimes creeps across her face. Even so, she keeps showing up: tired, but willing to try.

After her shifts, she joins the Rose of Bunkyo in protecting the ward's hidden side.

## In combat

Nono carried the **Supporter** role label in the October 2026 CBT — fitting for a nurse. See [combat roles](/guides/combat/#roles).
