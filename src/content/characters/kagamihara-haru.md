**Kagamihara Haru** (鏡原はる) is a member of the [Akina Urban Legend Research Institute](/factions/#akina-institute). She is voiced by **Iwami Manaka** (石見舞菜香).

## Profile

Haru lives a double life. Among her friends she is a sweet, delicate young lady. In the institute's clubroom she is a magician sprawled on the sofa watching **campy slasher films**.

Her favorite is the *Shinjuku Chinese Cleaver Incident* series — so much so that she **modeled her summon on the movie's slasher**. Her official introduction has her casually asking the [Shunin](/characters/shunin/) to fix her hair after another movie session.

## In combat

Haru carried the **Attacker** role label in the October 2026 CBT. Her official profile is the source for the slasher-inspired summon; exactly how it behaves in battle hasn't been officially documented yet. See [combat roles](/guides/combat/#roles).
