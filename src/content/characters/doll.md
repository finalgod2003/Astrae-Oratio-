**Doll** (人形) is a guardian created by Commissioner [Hiwagishiakari Ai](/characters/hiwagishiakari-ai/) to protect the [Shunin](/characters/shunin/). Doll is voiced by **Shiraishi Haruka** (白石晴香) — the same voice actress as the Commissioner.

## Profile

The official profile calls Doll a kind of **temporary bodyguard** for the Shunin, and refers to Doll simply as an "entity". The only hard fact given is that Doll's body is made of **wood**; everything else — how Doll was made, what Doll remembers, how much of a will of its own it has — is left open.

## How to get Doll

Doll is the one character whose acquisition the official CBT guidebook explained in detail:

1. Doll starts out **destroyed**. You restore Doll through the **Great Shrine** (called the Grand Temple in some CBT notices).
2. **Leveling up the Great Shrine** gradually repairs Doll.
3. Once restoration is complete, you are **guaranteed** to get Doll.
4. Doll **cannot be obtained from the gacha**.

During the CBT, the repair item was the **Oracle Device Daisy Wheel**, handed out 25 at a time in the daily login rewards. Treat the exact costs as CBT values — they may change at launch.

## In combat

In CBT footage Doll carried the **Attacker** role label. As a guaranteed unit, Doll is likely to be part of most new players' early teams. See the [combat guide](/guides/combat/) for how roles fit into the tag-based battle system.

## Trivia

- The CBT guidebook's cover art shows Doll curled up on a red sofa in the Special District Office.
- Doll and the Commissioner who built Doll share a voice actress. The game has not explained whether that means anything yet.
