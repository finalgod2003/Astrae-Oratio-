**Aizumi Yuzuki** (阿泉結月) is a member of the [YuLuKo Trio](/factions/#yuluko-trio). She is voiced by **Shimada Aino** (島田愛野).

## Profile

Yuzuki was born into an **ordinary Tokyo family** with no connection to witches whatsoever. That changed by pure coincidence, when she met a water spirit called **Rusalka** at a pond in her neighborhood. The encounter set her on the path of a witch.

Otherwise she is about as normal as they come: she loves **pro wrestling** and **romantasy novels**, and quietly admires the villainess archetype. She is also the type to get excited when a meeting feels like the start of a fated, manga-style bond.

## In the game

Yuzuki was **not** among the 18 characters playable in the October 2026 CBT footage. Her playable status has not been announced.
