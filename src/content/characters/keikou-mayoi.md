**Keikou Mayoi** (軽光真宵) is a member of the [Seiran Fellowship](/factions/#seiran-fellowship). She is voiced by **Omori Nichika** (大森日雅).

## Profile

Mayoi comes from one of **Japan's top ten corporate families**, and her magic matches her bank balance: she uses **jewel magic** that consumes — literally evaporates — expensive gemstones.

She wears braces and is not remotely self-conscious about them. Her manners can be rough and she gets overly familiar with people quickly, but she knows where the line is. When a situation calls for it, she can switch to a surprisingly polite and considerate side.

## In combat

Mayoi carried the **Breaker** role label in the October 2026 CBT. Gematsu's coverage of the September 2026 release-window announcement also singled her out as the Seiran Fellowship's gem-powered magician. See [combat roles](/guides/combat/#roles) and the [Break mechanic](/guides/combat/#break).
