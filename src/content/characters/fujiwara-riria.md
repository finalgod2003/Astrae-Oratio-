**Fujiwara Riria** (藤晴璃々愛) is a member of the [Magical Activities of the Minato Girls](/factions/#minato-girls). She is voiced by **Iryo Aira** (井料愛良).

> **Name note:** the official English site romanizes her name as "Fujiwara Riria", even though the Japanese surname is written 藤晴. This wiki follows the official English spelling.

## Profile

Riria is the daughter of an **extremely prestigious magician family** and an **ancient shrine maiden** who can wield the sorcery of the **Yamatai Kingdom**, the semi-legendary realm of early Japanese history.

For reasons the profile doesn't explain, she transferred into Minato Middle School. There she met [Tanaka Erin](/characters/tanaka-erin/) and [Anna M. Battenberg](/characters/anna-m-battenberg/), and she has been enjoying ordinary middle-school life ever since — with an enthusiasm for anything involving "magicians" and "girls".

## In combat

Riria carried the **Supporter** role label in the October 2026 CBT. She also co-hosts the game's training mode: the official CBT guidebook's Training screen is branded as Riria and Anna's after-school magic training. See [Training](/guides/progression/#training).

## Relationships

- [Tanaka Erin](/characters/tanaka-erin/) — the friend whose mythic magic Riria helped uncover.
- [Anna M. Battenberg](/characters/anna-m-battenberg/) — clanmate and training partner.
